@SandflySecurity Instead of making a copy of a binary, you can also use a command like 

$ exec -a ‘[kworkerd]’ /bin/sleep 600 &

The exe in /proc will point to /bin/sleep, but similarly shows up in ps as ‘[kworkerd]’

I tend to mount /temp as noexec to prevent these simple shenanigans.

**🌐 中文翻译**

与其复制二进制文件，你也可以使用类似这样的命令

$ exec -a ‘[kworkerd]’ /bin/sleep 600 &

`/proc` 中的 `exe` 将指向 `/bin/sleep`，但同样在 `ps` 中显示为 ‘[kworkerd]’

我倾向于将 `/temp` 挂载为 `noexec` 以防止这些简单的伎俩。
