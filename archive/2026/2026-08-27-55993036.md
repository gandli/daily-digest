Imagine your CCTV caught your partner sneaking out with someone… 👀

Instead of watching hours of footage, what if AI could spot and track people and vehicles for you?

This open-source project does exactly that.

It uses YOLOv5n6 to detect objects from live camera feeds in real time.

The clever part:

→ Processes low-res frames for speed
→ Maps detections onto high-res footage
→ Tracks people, cars, and other objects
→ Works with live CCTV feeds
Basically, your CCTV just got a computer vision upgrade.

**🌐 中文翻译**

想象一下，你的闭路电视摄像头拍到你的伴侣和某人偷偷溜出去……👀

与其花几个小时看录像，不如让人工智能帮你发现并追踪人和车辆呢？

巧妙之处在于：

→ 为速度处理低分辨率帧
→ 将检测结果映射到高分辨率录像上
→ 追踪人、汽车和其他物体
→ 支持实时闭路电视源
基本上，你的闭路电视刚刚升级了计算机视觉功能。
