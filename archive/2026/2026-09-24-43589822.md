I'd like to announce a new vulnerability chain we found that starts with an SVG parser bug. It affects multiple products and can lead to RCE.

I created http://github.com/rafabd1/VectorFreed to share more about the chain, which I'm calling "VectorFreed" for now.

I started looking into this early this month and have since confirmed command execution in several products that process SVGs.  The root issue is CVE-2026-96889, with fixes in librsvg 2.63.2 and the 2.62.4 backport.

The repo has an early overview and references for now. I'll share the technical write-up in the coming weeks.
