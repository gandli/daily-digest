I’ve alway’s thought it’s a shame that WebAssembly has the word “web” in it. Gives it a connotation that it’s “only for browsers” or something.

Meanwhile, NASA’s over here with a flight-compliant derivative (SpaceWASM) intended for spacecraft!

The intent is writing mission software to a standardized bytecode, where the end platform / ISA is otherwise arbitrary. In other words, run the same mission package on ARM, Rad-hard PowerPC, RISC-V, whatever.

Thus, after-flight you could (safely) upload new payloads with “approved” APIs.

Weird to think about, but just like buggy WASM code shouldn’t crash your browser…the same logic applies to not crashing your critical flight hardware.

**🌐 中文翻译**

我一直觉得 WebAssembly 这个名字里带“web”这个词真是个遗憾。它让人觉得它“只适用于浏览器”之类的东西。

与此同时，NASA 那边搞出了一个符合飞行标准的衍生版本（SpaceWASM），专门用于航天器！

其意图是将任务软件编写成标准字节码形式，最终平台 / ISA 则可以是任意的。换句话说，在 ARM、辐射加固 PowerPC、RISC-V 或其他任何平台上运行相同的任务包。

因此，飞行结束后，你可以（安全地）上传带有“已批准”API 的新负载。

想想有点奇怪，但就像有 bug 的 WASM 代码不应该崩溃你的浏览器一样……同样的逻辑也适用于不崩溃你的关键飞行硬件。
