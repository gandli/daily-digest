Holy shit... Someone built a free AI that watches your security cameras and describes what happened in plain english.

It's called Clearcam. It bolts onto any security camera you already own and turns it into a smart one.

No new hardware. No cloud. The AI runs on your own machine.

→ Spots and tracks people, cars, and objects the moment they appear
→ Saves a clip of the exact event instead of 24 hours of an empty driveway
