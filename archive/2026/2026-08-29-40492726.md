PwnEye  

PwnEye can test cameras through ONVIF and RTSP. It can play the stream, deface a camera, move it and get a shell.  

Cameras shouldn't be ignored during a pentest. There are dozens of known CVEs affecting them. They can be used as proxy to attack other hosts or maintain persistence. IoT devices can cause havoc if you don't update them.
