Making a letter-size trail map for an orienteering event (find checkpoints), min shade and steep grades...

Since this map's for use at a trot, kept it simple: trail names, fences, elevation contours, benches, creeks, water tanks

Trails you get via OpenStreetMap or Overture Maps; I also like to overlay the official county trail maps, Claude can georeference arbitrary images if it sees good intersections

And remember, ABC: Always Be stealing Commercial imagery via QGIS "save as" so you can pick out the fine details for tracing/confirming ratty little use trails

为定向越野赛事（寻找检查点）制作一封A4尺寸的步道地图，最小化阴影和陡坡……

由于这份地图用于小跑时使用，我保持了简洁：步道名称、围栏、高程等高线、长椅、小溪、水箱

步道数据可通过 OpenStreetMap 或 Overture Maps 获取；我也喜欢叠加官方县步道地图，Claude 可以地理参照任意图像，只要它能看到良好的交叉点

还有，记住 ABC：Always Be stealing 商业图像 via QGIS "save as"，这样你就能挑出精细细节用于描迹/确认那些破烂的小用步道
