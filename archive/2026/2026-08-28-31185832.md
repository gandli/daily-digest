Your documents are full of facts. Most tools still treat them like blobs.

Hyper-Extract is an LLM-powered knowledge-extraction CLI for builders who want to turn unstructured text into structured Knowledge Abstracts.

It helps you move from a document to a queryable, visualizable output by pairing a command-line parse flow with templates for structured extraction.

Key features:

• One-command workflow – parse a document, then query or visualize the extracted result with the CLI
• Eight knowledge structures – choose from lists and Pydantic models through graphs, hypergraphs, and spatio-temporal graphs
• 80+ YAML templates – start with presets across finance, legal, medical, industry, and general domains
• Multiple extraction engines – includes GraphRAG, LightRAG, Hyper-RAG, KG-Gen, and more
• Local model option – configure local vLLM with Qwen3.5-9B and bge-m3 for extraction and embeddings

It’s open-source (Apache License 2.0).

• 80+ YAML 模板 – 从金融、法律、医疗、行业和通用领域的预设模板开始
• 多种提取引擎 – 包括 GraphRAG、LightRAG、Hyper-RAG、KG-Gen 等
• 本地模型选项 – 配置本地 vLLM，使用 Qwen3.5-9B 和 bge-m3 进行提取和嵌入

它是开源的（Apache License 2.0）。
