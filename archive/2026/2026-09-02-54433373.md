Which object tracker should you use? Speed or stable IDs?

Someone compared several trackers recently added to the Ultralytics ecosystem, including OCSORT, DeepOCSORT, FastTrack and TrackTrack, alongside BoT-SORT and ByteTrack.

TrackTrack and BoT-SORT stood out for the most stable tracking IDs.

ByteTrack and FastTrack were the speed champions, while still maintaining good tracking accuracy.

So it really comes down to your use case.

Need stable identities across frames, go with TrackTrack or BoT-SORT.

Need maximum speed for real-time applications, ByteTrack or FastTrack could be the better choice.

There is no single best tracker. The best one depends on whether your project values speed, accuracy, or keeping the same ID locked to an object.
