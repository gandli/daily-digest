We shipped Tailscale as a worker in iii.

Not “remote access for the harness.” @Tailscale itself is another worker like harness.

Peers, Serve, Funnel, Taildrop, exit nodes, certs, Taildrive. All exposed through the worker, so the agent can actually use the network instead of just connecting through it.

Scan a QR code on your phone and you’re back on the same harness.

No separate mobile harness. No remote desktop setup.

Just your harness, wherever you are.
